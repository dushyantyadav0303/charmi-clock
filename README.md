## About the Project
### **Charmi Clock** - it is fully working 3d printable cuckoo clock design in fusion 360. it having small mechanical bird that make cuckoo sound every hour.
## why i made this?
### While Doom Scrolling I saw a Traditional Cuckoo clock and the next Second I love this Clock. But it is not available in India!!!. So I Think why not to 3D printed but there isn't 3D model available on Internet. So I thought why not to make our own. And make it open source so everyone can enjoy this cuckoo clock.
